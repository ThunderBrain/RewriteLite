How it works

From a sentence to a finished draft.

Rewrite a selection

Highlight a passage and open the floating editor. Pick a writing style, edit the result, then replace the selection in supported controls or copy it anywhere.

View example
Input
With regard to the budget, I was thinking that perhaps we could consider moving our meeting to tomorrow.
Result
Could we move our budget meeting to tomorrow?

Compose from an idea

With double Ctrl enabled, press it with no text selected. Describe what you want to say in your own language. Compose uses the output language you chose in Settings.

View example
Input
给 Mia 写封简短的邮件，感谢她整理笔记，并告诉她我周四会发初稿。
Result
Hi Mia, thanks for putting the notes together. I’ll send you a first draft on Thursday.

Refine a draft

Ask for a warmer tone, a shorter ending, or one more detail. Refine remembers the current draft and its instructions. Starting a new task gives you a fresh context.

View example
Input
Could we move our budget meeting to tomorrow?
Follow-up
Suggest a time and make it more casual.
Result
Does 2 tomorrow work for a quick budget chat?

FAQ

Language, style and models

Can I give instructions in one language and draft in another?

Yes. Set your Compose target language in Settings, then describe the recipient, key points and tone in whichever language you prefer. Compose remembers your choice for the next draft.

Can I save a writing style I use often?

Open Writing styles on the main screen, name your style and describe it. Try “Keep sentences short and preserve technical terms.” You can choose that style when rewriting or composing.

How can I write with a local model?

Start Ollama with a model on your computer. Connect to its local address in RewriteLite, choose the model and start writing.
